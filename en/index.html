Process

From enquiry to final footage

Every project follows the same proven flight plan — no surprises.

STAGE 01

Enquiry

You send the location, preferred date and the goal of the footage. I reply within 24 hours.

STAGE 02

Flight plan

I check the airspace, the weather forecast and the light, then prepare a precise shot list.

STAGE 03

Shoot

On location I shoot 4K material with a stabilised gimbal — following the agreed plan.

STAGE 04

Post-production

Colour grading, editing and export in formats for web, ads and social media.

STAGE 05

Delivery

You receive the final files via a download link — usually 3 to 5 days after the shoot.
