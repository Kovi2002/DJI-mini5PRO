2. What data I collect and why

When you submit the booking form, I receive the details you enter: full name, email address, phone number (optional), preferred date, project type, approximate budget (optional), and location and notes.

I use the data solely to answer your enquiry, prepare a quote and carry out the agreed shoot. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) and taking steps at your request prior to entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

I do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or pass it on to third parties other than the processors listed below.

3. Processors

  • Formspree (Formspree, Inc., USA) — forwards the form content to my email address. In doing so, data is also processed in the USA. Formspree privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) — website hosting. On each visit GitHub logs technical data such as your IP address for security and operation. GitHub privacy statement.

Fonts, scripts, images and videos are loaded directly from the kovifly.si server — the site does not call external services for fonts, analytics or advertising. The WhatsApp and Instagram links take you to those providers' sites, where their own terms apply.

4. Retention

I keep enquiries for as long as communication about the project lasts, and for no more than 12 months after the last contact. Data linked to issued invoices is kept for as long as the law requires.

5. Your rights

At any time you can request access to your data, its rectification or erasure, restriction of processing or data portability, object to processing, or withdraw your consent. Simply write to me. If you believe your rights have been infringed, you can lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia.

6. Cookies

The kovifly.si site does not use cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking, which is why there is no cookie banner. The site stores nothing in your browser.
