[[Preden vzletiva||Before we take off]]

[[Cena je odvisna od lokacije, trajanja snemanja in obsega montaže. Pošlji povpraševanje z nekaj podatki o projektu in v 24 urah prejmeš okvirno ponudbo — brez obveznosti.||The price depends on the location, the length of the shoot and how much editing is needed. Send an enquiry with a few details about your project and you'll get an estimate within 24 hours — no obligation.]]

[[Dron ne leti v dežju in močnem vetru. Napoved spremljam nekaj dni vnaprej; če razmere niso varne ali svetloba ni primerna, se dogovoriva za nov termin.||The drone doesn't fly in rain or strong wind. I follow the forecast a few days ahead; if conditions aren't safe or the light isn't right, we agree on a new date.]]

[[To preverim jaz. Pred vsakim snemanjem pregledam zračni prostor in omejitve lokacije ter ti sporočim, ali je let mogoč in pod kakšnimi pogoji.||I check that for you. Before every shoot I review the airspace and the restrictions of the location and let you know whether the flight is possible and under what conditions.]]

[[Končne datoteke običajno prejmeš v 3 do 5 dneh po snemanju, prek povezave za prenos.||You usually receive the final files 3 to 5 days after the shoot, via a download link.]]
